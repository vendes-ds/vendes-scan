Vendes Scan – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Vendes Scan is an internal warehouse tool of Vendes Corporation (Philippines). It is used only by authorized Vendes staff to scan parcels for picking, packing and dispatch.

What we access

  • Your Google account email and name, to sign you in and record who made each scan.
  • Google Sheets owned by Vendes Corporation, to read the item list and save scan records. The app does not read your other files.

How data is used and stored

Scan records (waybill number, time, stage, and the staff email) are saved only in Vendes Corporation's Google Sheets. Scans made offline are kept on your phone until they sync. We do not sell or share your data, and we do not use it for ads.

Removing access

You can remove the app's access anytime at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To delete your records, contact us.
